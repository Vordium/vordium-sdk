# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in the Vordium network, the Vordex app, the SDKs, the AI server or the Vordium websites,
report it privately to **security@vordium.com**.

Do not open a public issue, and do not publish or share the details until the issue is fixed.

## What to include

- what you found and what it lets someone do;
- the steps to reproduce it, and a proof of concept where you have one;
- the network, the address or page, and the version you tested.

## How to test

- Test on the testnet (https://docs.vordium.com/testnet/) or on a local node, never on mainnet.
- Use only your own accounts. Do not access other people's accounts or funds, and do not degrade the service for
  others.

## After you report

The issue is fixed first; the details are published after the fix is live.

What is proven about Vordium's protections today: https://github.com/Vordium/security
